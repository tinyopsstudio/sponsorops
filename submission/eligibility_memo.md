## New business test

SponsorOps is a new B2B sponsorship-operations business workflow created in July 2026, during the May 19 through August 17 submission period. It sells sponsored-content placement and fulfillment to relevant advertisers while using AI agents to operate the sponsor pipeline.

TinyOps Studio LLC and the Troutmate publishing asset existed before the submission period. They are disclosed as pre-existing entity and operating resources. The SponsorOps business, sponsor-sales controller, sponsor-fit policy, advertiser workflow, fulfillment controller, case study, and Gemini decision service were created during the submission period.

## Business activity and revenue

SponsorOps provides sponsored-content placement and fulfillment to advertisers for financial payment. Its first deployment generated one new arms-length advertiser customer and $99 in settled revenue on 2026-07-23. The customer was not a team member, family member, related entity, or pre-existing TinyOps customer.

Related-party revenue: $0.

Paid marketing and customer-acquisition spend: $0.

## Required platform use

The deployed service is designed for Cloud Run and uses Gemini through Vertex AI. Cloud Build, Artifact Registry, and Cloud Logging support deployment and production evidence.

## Privacy and evidence handling

The initial submission will use anonymized customer evidence because the customer has not consented to sharing personal contact details with judges. The XPRIZE FAQ allows names that cannot be shared to be crossed out or anonymized. If judges request direct customer contact information later, TinyOps will seek customer consent before disclosure.

The corporate ID and transaction evidence will be submitted privately. Public source and videos will exclude owner identifiers, bank data, email addresses, phone numbers, and private customer contact data.
